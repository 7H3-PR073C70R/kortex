Socratic Tutor

Meet Syllabot, the tutor that asks you back.

Instead of just handing you the answer, Syllabot nudges you to think it through, so it stays with you on exam day. When typing feels like too much, you can ask out loud with your voice.

🧭 Step-by-step hints 🎙️ Ask by voice 📚 Knows your syllabus
SYLLABOT AI
Online now
You: "I keep mixing up when to use Integration by Parts versus plain Substitution."
Syllabot:
"Good question. Look at the shape of the problem. If one part is the derivative of the other, substitution is usually faster. But when you have x multiplied by e to the 2x, what happens if you take the derivative of just the x?"
You: "It becomes 1, so the messy part goes away?"
Syllabot:
"Exactly. That is your signal for Integration by Parts. Try setting it up and tell me what you pick for u."
