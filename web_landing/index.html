CBT QUESTION

JAMB CBT Question: A car starts from rest and accelerates uniformly at 2.5 m/s² for 8 seconds. Calculate the total distance covered by the car.

CORRECT OPTION & EXPLANATION

Option C (80m) is Correct:
Using s = ut + ½at² where initial speed u = 0, acceleration a = 2.5 m/s², and time t = 8s:
s = 0(8) + ½(2.5)(8)² = ½(2.5)(64) = 80 meters.
