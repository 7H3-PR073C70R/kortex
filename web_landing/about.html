The Background

Why passive studying fails modern curricula

Every student knows the feeling: sitting through hundreds of disorganized PDF slides, highlighting textbooks in five neon colors, and still feeling completely unprepared when faced with a real exam question.

Decades of cognitive science research (including pioneering work by Ebbinghaus, Bjork, and Dunlosky) demonstrate that human retention requires two essential mechanics: effortful active retrieval and dynamically spaced review right before memory decay sets in.

Kortexify automates these mechanics directly from your raw course materials. Whether you are reading anatomy lecture decks for a medical degree, solving multivariable calculus in engineering, mastering accounting standards for ICAN, or drilling past CBT questions for JAMB and SAT, Kortexify extracts atomic flashcards and schedules review intervals with mathematical precision.

Product Philosophy

Four non-negotiable standards

🎴

1. Cognitive Active Retrieval

No passive scrolling or false feelings of competence. Every concept is tested through two-sided recall, LaTeX math rendering, and multi-tier interval adjustments that adapt to your personal recall speed.

🤖

2. Grounded Socratic Syllabot

Unlike generic AI tools that hallucinate plausible-sounding answers, Syllabot anchors its explanations directly to your uploaded course documents, lecture notes, and syllabus outlines, guiding you step-by-step through first principles.

⏱️

3. Real Exam-Condition CBT

Mastering theory is only half the battle. Our timed test engine replicates realistic computer-based testing environments for standardized exams and university midterms, complete with instant review and score breakdowns.

🔒

4. Student Privacy & Data Ownership

Your notes and study progress belong to you. Your cards and library remain accessible offline on desktop and web, synchronized securely with encrypted endpoints and zero third-party marketing trackers.
