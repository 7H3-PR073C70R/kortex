Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 31, 2026 • Effective Date: September 1, 2026

Our Core Privacy Commitment:

Kortexify is built by students, for students. We never sell your personal data, lecture notes, or study cards to third-party advertisers. Your uploaded PDFs and course materials are strictly used to power your personal study sessions and RAG vectors.

1. Information We Collect

When you use Kortexify, we collect information necessary to provide and improve our cognitive study services:

  • Account Information: Name, university or personal email address, and authentication credentials.
  • Study Materials & User Content: Uploaded PDF documents, syllabi, notes, generated flashcards, and review history (intervals, stability, retrievability logs).
  • Usage & Device Telemetry: Anonymized app crash logs, device model, operating system version, and general interaction metrics to maintain high-performance SLAs.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is processed strictly for the following purposes:

  • Synthesizing personalized active recall flashcards from your uploaded coursework.
  • Calculating deterministic spaced repetition intervals via the FSRS v4 memory algorithm.
  • Synchronizing your decks and review states seamlessly across iOS, Android, Desktop, and Web.
  • Facilitating optional collaborative study rooms and course community forums.

3. AI Processing & Third-Party LLMs

To extract LaTeX equations and generate Socratic questions, your text chunks are processed through secure, enterprise-grade AI gateways. Our model providers operate under strict zero-retention agreements: your data is never used to train public foundation models.

4. Data Storage, Encryption & Security

All data in transit is protected using TLS 1.3 encryption. Data at rest in our Supabase Postgres database is secured with AES-256 encryption and granular Row Level Security (RLS) policies, guaranteeing that only your authenticated account can access your cards.

5. Your Rights & Data Portability

You have total control over your academic data. At any time, you may:

  • Export all your flashcards in standard Anki (.apkg), Notion CSV, or PDF format.
  • Request a complete copy of all personal data stored in your account.
  • Permanently delete your account and all associated documents with a single click.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or privacy requests, please contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@kortexify.app or visit our Contact Page.
