Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026 • Effective Date: September 2026

Our Core Privacy Commitment:

Kortexify is built around offline-first architecture. We never sell your personal data, lecture handouts, or study cards to third-party advertisers. Your uploaded PDFs, notes, and formulas are processed locally or through zero-retention private pipelines strictly to power your study decks.

1. Information We Collect

When you use Kortexify, we only store data required to run your personalized spaced repetition engine:

  • Account Information (Optional): If you choose to sync across devices, your email and authentication credentials.
  • Study Materials & Notes: Uploaded PDF documents, syllabi, notes, generated flashcards, and review history (intervals, stability, retrievability logs).
  • Local Storage: All cards and FSRS states live primarily in your on-device SQLite database.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is processed strictly for cognitive service delivery:

  • Generating active recall flashcards, LaTeX formulas, and Socratic hints via Syllabot AI.
  • Calculating personalized FSRS-6 review intervals based on your historical memory stability.
  • Synchronizing your decks and offline study progress across your authorized devices.

3. AI Processing & Privacy Safeguards

When extracting LaTeX equations or generating Socratic prompts, content is parsed locally whenever device capability permits. Cloud fallbacks operate under strict zero-retention agreements: your study materials are never used to train public models.

4. Data Storage & Security

All data in transit is encrypted using modern TLS 1.3. On-device databases are isolated within your operating system sandbox. Any cloud synchronization utilizes AES-256 encryption with granular Row Level Security (RLS) policies.

5. Your Rights & Data Portability

You own your academic materials. At any time, you can:

  • Export all your decks in standard Anki (.apkg), Markdown, or PDF format.
  • Delete your local or cloud database with a single tap.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or privacy inquiries, contact our team at privacy@kortexify.app or visit our Contact Page.
