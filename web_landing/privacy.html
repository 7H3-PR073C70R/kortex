Data Security & Privacy

Your notes belong to you. Period.

Last updated: October 2026. Review how Kortexify enforces offline-first data protection, encrypted synchronization, and zero-retention AI pipelines.

Core Privacy Pledge: Kortexify operates under an offline-first architecture. We do not sell your personal data, uploaded syllabi, lecture decks, or active recall cards to third-party ad networks. Your academic files are parsed strictly to generate your personal study tools.

1. Information We Collect

We deliberately minimize data collection to the minimum required to power spaced repetition and synchronization:

  • Account Credentials: If you create a cross-device sync account, we store your email address and cryptographic authentication tokens.
  • Study Content & Academic Materials: Notes, syllabus PDFs, generated flashcards, and review performance logs (FSRS retrievability, difficulty factor, and repetition counts).
  • Local Storage State: On native desktop apps, your entire library is stored in local SQLite databases sandboxed by your operating system.

2. Grounded AI Processing & Model Safeguards

When you ask Syllabot to generate flashcards or explain a derivation from lecture slides:

  • Content is processed using private API endpoints with zero-retention service agreements.
  • Your proprietary coursework, exam notes, and student research are never used to train public machine learning foundation models.
  • All extracted LaTeX formulas and card decks remain under your complete ownership.

3. Data Storage, Encryption & Sync

All network transmissions are strictly encrypted via TLS 1.3. When cloud backup is active, cloud records are protected by AES-256 encryption at rest, fortified with granular Row Level Security (RLS) policies guaranteeing that only your authenticated user ID can decrypt your study decks.

4. Data Portability & Deletion

You can export your cards and study progress at any time into open formats including standard Anki packages (.apkg), Markdown, and JSON. If you delete your account, all associated server backups are purged permanently.

5. Inquiries & Data Requests

For questions or data access requests, contact our privacy officer directly at privacy@kortexify.app or via our Contact page.
