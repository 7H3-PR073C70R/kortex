Action Potential Chapter 03 · Slide 14

A rapid, temporary reversal of the electrical potential across a neuronal plasma membrane, driven by sequential opening of voltage-gated Na⁺ and K⁺ channels.

Synaptic Plasticity Chapter 06 · Slide 32

The biological capacity of synapses to strengthen (LTP) or weaken (LTD) over time in response to increases or decreases in synaptic activity, forming the physical basis of learning.

Myelin Sheath & Saltatory Conduction Chapter 04 · Slide 21

Insulating lipid layer formed by oligodendrocytes (CNS) or Schwann cells (PNS) that enables nerve impulses to jump rapidly between Nodes of Ranvier.
