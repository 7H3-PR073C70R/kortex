1. Apply Integration by Parts formula: $\int u \, dv = uv - \int v \, du$

$= [-x \cos(x)]_{0}^{\pi} - \int_{0}^{\pi} (-\cos(x)) \, dx = (-\pi(-1) - 0) + [\sin(x)]_{0}^{\pi} = \pi + 0 = \pi$.
