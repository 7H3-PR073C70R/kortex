Can I pay with a Nigerian bank debit card?

Yes. Select Naira (₦) using the currency toggle at the top of this page. You can pay seamlessly in Naira with any local Mastercard, Visa, or Verve card, or use direct bank transfer with instant automated activation.

How does the free starter plan work?

You can create an account and immediately upload up to 5 course decks without entering any credit card information. Your spaced-repetition schedules and progress remain yours permanently.

Is Kortexify suitable for university midterms and exam prep?

Kortexify is specifically calibrated for university coursework (BSc, HND, OND), post-graduate research (MSc, PhD), and standardized entrance exams (JAMB/UTME, WAEC, SAT). You drop your real syllabus materials, and Syllabot tests you strictly on what your professor expects.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

Yes, you can cancel your subscription with a single click inside your account settings. You will retain full access until the end of your billing cycle with zero cancellation fees.
